The story

Why now

Panamá is widely credited as the birthplace of reggae en español, the sound that grew into reggaeton. Udini La Voz is a Panamanian artist who has spent fifteen years earning stages in the United States and Panamá, collaborated with Michael D. Ellis, an originator of reggaeton, and in 2026 performed on the FIFA Fan Festival main stage and closed the Premios Hispanos de Delaware. Year fifteen is the break-out push, built around one record.

Short bio

Udini La Voz (Odin Palacio) is a Panamanian recording artist based in Philadelphia who raps in Spanish and English. With three albums, a mixtape and the anthem "We Go Hard (Latino)," he has performed at the FIFA Fan Festival and closed the Premios Hispanos de Delaware.

Full bio

Born in Panama City and based in Philadelphia, Udini La Voz (Odin Palacio) raps in Spanish and English with one mission: unite cultures. He has released three albums (Me and My Musika, The Passport and the double album Barrio Love) plus a mixtape, and collaborated with Michael D. Ellis, an originator of reggaeton. His stages include the FIFA Fan Festival in Philadelphia, Wawa Welcome America, the Recording Academy Philadelphia Chapter's Música y Sabor, World Cafe Live and the Panamanian Day Parade in Brooklyn. In 2026 he closed the Premios Hispanos de Delaware with his anthem "We Go Hard (Latino)." On screen he has appeared in CREED II and in campaigns for the Philadelphia 76ers, Hulu and AT&T, and he has hosted bilingual television and radio. He is also the founder of La Torche LLC, a youth development company.
