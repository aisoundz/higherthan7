Playbook · Part 1

Six moves, in order

Every solo founder has the same three problems: no audience, no recurring revenue, no time. Each step below solves one of them, and each one feeds the next.

1

Own the brand

Register the entity, lock the logo, colors and type. HT7 already has a full style guide, so every product looks like one company.

Cost: one time
2

Lead with a product

Two singles by Udini La Voz, released June 17, 2026 on HT7. Music is the cheapest way to get discovered and the easiest thing to share.

Job: get attention
3

Add recurring revenue

HigherThan7.com at $8.99 a month. Subscriptions pay you every month without a new sale, which is what makes cash flow predictable.

Job: keep the fan
4

Sell without inventory

Print-on-demand merch through Printify. Nothing is printed until someone pays, so there is no stock to buy and no garage full of boxes.

Job: raise order value
5

Capture every fan

Every link points to one email list. Social platforms rent you an audience. An email list is the audience you own.

Job: own the relationship
6

Watch the cash weekly

Separate business account, one sheet, one number: did cash go up this week? Cut what does not pay back within 90 days.

Job: stay alive
Song→ Email list→ Subscription→ Merch drop→ Next song
