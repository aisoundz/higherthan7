The story

A voice that crosses borders

Odín Palacio grew up in Panama City, Panamá. Today, as Udini La Voz, he is a fixture of Latino entertainment from Philadelphia to New York, and his reach keeps growing.

He records in Spanish and English, and he collaborated with Michael D. Ellis, an originator of reggaeton. He has performed at the FIFA Fan Fest and Philadelphia's Fourth of July celebration, closed the Delaware Hispanic Awards, and taken the stage in Panamá. He has also appeared in CREED II and in national campaigns alongside Joel Embiid, Ben Simmons and Jay Wright.

He hosts on television and radio, walks runways, and runs his own company for young people. Everything he does carries the same message: Latino unity, Panamanian pride and a seat at the international table.

The vision · 10 Años

Ten years of building. Now breaking.

Udini has spent a decade earning every stage he has stood on. Year ten is about taking Panamá's sound to the world. This is the plan.

  1. 01

    One anthem, everywhere

    Make "We Go Hard" the Latino game-day chant: stadiums, gyms and creator videos, one hook at a time.

  2. 02

    New music every six weeks

    A steady run of releases in Spanish and English, each with short vertical videos made to be shared.

  3. 03

    Panamá to the world

    Collaborations with rising and established Latin artists, starting with Panamá's own scene.

  4. 04

    Big moments, big screens

    Sync for sports and brand campaigns, festival stages, and the 10 Años live show.
